Dreyvis Connectors: pick a look

Six directions for the Connectors page, and the same look carries to the buttons you press from the orb. Every preview is real working CSS with your actual connectors in it (no logos, just each app's colour and initial). Moving parts hold still if your Mac has Reduce Motion on.

1. Orbit Station My pick

Your apps orbit a little copy of the Dreyvis orb. Connected ones glow in their brand colour, waiting ones pulse, and apps you haven't added sit dim on the outer ring. It feels like Dreyvis's own world, not a settings screen.

Background: deep navy with a soft blue spotlight
Surfaces: glowing round "planets", pill buttons
Type: Space Grotesk, rounded and techy
Layout: orbit up top, add-and-search list below
Motion: rings turn slowly, waiting apps pulse. Still with Reduce Motion
Why it fits: it reuses the orb, so the app feels like one thing
Tradeoff: past ~20 apps the orbit gets busy, so extras go in the list
Reference: Apple Vision Pro glowing depth, plus your own orb

2. Home Screen

Every connector is a big colourful app icon, like your iPhone home screen. A green dot means connected and yellow means it's waiting for you. Icons you haven't added are grey until you tap them.

Background: blue-to-indigo gradient
Surfaces: rounded app icons with soft shadows
Type: Outfit, friendly and round
Layout: icon grid with folders (Work, Social, Money)
Motion: icons tilt up on hover. Still with Reduce Motion
Why it fits: anyone gets it instantly, which helps when you show friends
Tradeoff: less room to explain what each app does
Reference: Raycast Store icon grid, iOS home screen

3. Aurora Glass

Frosted glass cards float over slow blue and teal northern lights. It's calm and premium, and each card still says in plain words what the app lets Dreyvis do.

Background: near-black with drifting blue/teal light
Surfaces: frosted glass with real blur
Type: Sora, clean and modern
Layout: card grid, status in colour words
Motion: the light drifts slowly. Still with Reduce Motion
Why it fits: keeps the useful descriptions and adds atmosphere
Tradeoff: the closest to what you have now, just prettier
Reference: Linear integrations card layout, macOS glass

4. Patch Panel

An old studio switchboard. Each app is a jack, and connecting one lights it up in that app's colour. Jacks waiting on you blink amber. It's physical and playful, like gear in a recording studio.

Background: graphite with faint rack lines
Surfaces: metal panel, sunken jacks, masking-tape labels
Type: IBM Plex Mono, studio-gear feel
Layout: jack grid wired to a Dreyvis box
Motion: waiting jacks blink. Still with Reduce Motion
Why it fits: "plugging Dreyvis into things" is literally what this page does
Tradeoff: most stylised, and the mono font reads a bit techy
Reference: Teenage Engineering OP-1 hardware playfulness

5. Daylight Bento

A bright, clean light version. A big tile shows how many apps are connected, and the rest sit in friendly white tiles with coloured tags. This is the only light option, for when the dark look feels heavy.

Background: soft cool grey
Surfaces: white rounded tiles, gentle shadow
Type: Plus Jakarta Sans, bold and friendly
Layout: bento grid with one hero number tile
Motion: the count rolls up when it changes. Still with Reduce Motion
Why it fits: easiest to read in daylight and on a phone
Tradeoff: breaks from the dark orb, so the app feels like two styles
Reference: Vercel integrations clarity, Apple bento grids

6. Shelves

Laid out like a streaming app: rows you scroll sideways, called "Connected", "Waiting for you", "Popular" and "From Claude Code". Big colourful tiles grow when you hover. It's fun to browse when you're looking for something new to add.

Background: deep navy fade
Surfaces: big gradient tiles in each app's colour
Type: Fredoka, playful and rounded
Layout: horizontal rows by group
Motion: tiles grow on hover, rows slide. Still with Reduce Motion
Why it fits: 1,000 apps feel like a catalogue to explore, not a list
Tradeoff: side-scrolling rows are harder with a keyboard
Reference: Netflix rows, App Store Today shelves
