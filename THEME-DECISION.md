# Theme decision

- Project and purpose: Dreyvis Connectors page (`/connectors`) and the nav buttons on the orb. Connect apps and custom MCP servers to Dreyvis.
- Audience and main action: Drey (and friends he shares Dreyvis with). Main action: connect an app.
- User-selected direction and date: "A cross between 3 and 6" from `docs/theme-board/connectors-board.html`, 2026-11-18.
- Primary structure: 6, Shelves. Rows by group (Waiting for you, Connected, Needs sign-in again, Popular, From Claude Code, Search), scrolled sideways.
- Secondary accents: 3, Aurora Glass. Near-black background with drifting blue and teal light, frosted glass tiles and panels, each tile tinted by its app's colour.
- Palette and contrast targets: background #040914; text #e8eefc; muted #a9bcdc; accent #5aa0ff; ok #6dffc8; waiting #ffd76d. Body text at least 4.5:1 on glass.
- Typography: Sora, 400 and 600 only. Body 16px, headings 24/32/40.
- Image and motion treatment: no logos, only each app's colour and initial. Motion comes from Drey Motion 12 (@zeeuiux), played once: Spotlight Entrance for the title, Proof in Motion for the connected count (real data only), and Success Bloom only after the backend confirms a connection. The aurora drifts slowly. Everything holds still under prefers-reduced-motion.
- References: Netflix and App Store shelves for the rows; Linear integrations and macOS glass for the cards.
- Existing content/functionality to preserve: Composio add, reconnect and waiting states, search across all apps, custom connector form, remove, and the restart-brain prompt.
- Verification plan: phone (375) and desktop widths; keyboard through every row; reduced motion; both inspectors; the cognitive-load check.
- Remaining unknowns: whether Ethan's dashboard should get the same look (not part of this change).

Approval applies to this project, not automatically to future websites.
